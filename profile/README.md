# MintoCred

**Compare personal loan offers from multiple lending partners through one simple online journey.**

MintoCred helps users explore personalised loan offers, compare available options, complete digital KYC, and track their loan application journey.

> **Website:** https://mintocred.com

## About MintoCred

MintoCred is designed to make the loan discovery and application process easier to understand and navigate. Users can submit basic details, review eligible offers from lending partners, choose an option, and continue with the lender's digital application and verification process.

Loan approval, interest rates, fees, eligibility, and disbursal are subject to the applicable lender's policies and final assessment.

## How it works

1. **Apply for a loan** — Enter the basic details requested to check eligibility and receive available pre-approval information.
2. **Compare offers** — Review the personalised loan offers made available through participating lenders.
3. **Choose and apply** — Select an offer and complete the lender's required application and digital KYC steps.
4. **Receive funds** — If the lender approves the application and completes its requirements, the loan amount is disbursed directly to the applicant's bank account by the lender.

## Key features

- **Online application journey** — Start the loan application process online.
- **Offer comparison** — Review available rates and EMI details side by side.
- **Digital KYC** — Complete verification digitally where supported by the lender.
- **Application tracking** — Follow the status of submitted applications.
- **Loan management features** — The planned mobile experience includes application tracking, offer comparison, EMI reminders, and approval updates.

Availability of features and offers may vary by user, lender, and location.

## Important information about loans

MintoCred helps users discover and compare loan offers. It should not be assumed that every applicant will receive an offer or approval.

Before accepting a loan, review the lender's complete terms, including:

- Annual Percentage Rate (APR)
- Interest rate and whether it is fixed or variable
- Processing fees and other applicable charges
- Loan tenure and EMI amount
- Repayment schedule and late-payment charges
- Foreclosure or prepayment conditions

For details about how interest and fees are calculated, refer to the APR disclosure available through the official MintoCred website.

## Safety and privacy

Use only the official MintoCred website and verified communication channels when submitting information or following an application. Review the applicable privacy policy and lender terms before sharing personal or financial information.

Never share your passwords, PINs, or one-time passwords (OTPs) with anyone claiming to represent MintoCred or a lending partner.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does MintoCred guarantee loan approval?

No. Any pre-approval or offer indication is not a guarantee of final approval. The lending partner makes the final eligibility and approval decision.

### Will I receive offers from every listed lending partner?

Not necessarily. Offers depend on the information provided, eligibility checks, and each lender's policies.

### How will I receive the loan amount?

If the selected lender approves the application and completes its requirements, the lender disburses the funds directly to the applicant's bank account.
